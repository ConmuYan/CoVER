# Paper Figures + Tables — TKDE 2026 (3-contribution submission)

Publication-grade figures and tables for the three independent contributions
(C1 RAER + C2 LREE + C3 Flash-RAER+CBR). Generated by
`scripts/build_paper_figures.py` from the 938-run experimental evidence base.

All figures are exported in three formats:
- `.pdf` — editable text (`pdf.fonttype=42`), preferred for journal upload
- `.svg` — vector source for last-mile edits (Inkscape / Illustrator)
- `.png` — 600 dpi raster preview

All tables are exported in three formats:
- `.csv` — machine-readable (pandas, R, Excel)
- `.md` — paper-readable Markdown
- `.tex` — LaTeX `booktabs` ready for direct `\input{}` into the manuscript

Style: Nature Machine Intelligence pastel — Arial 7 pt, restrained palette
(neutral grey + signal blue/teal + accent orange/red + sig gold).

### Figure 5 (`c3_cbr/fig5_c3_ablation_matrix`) — C3 7-axis ablation heatmap

**Suggested caption**:

> **C3 — full ablation matrix: per-cell Δ AUPRC vs the deterministic-mask
> Flash-RAER baseline across all 21 tested variants (5-seed mean per
> cell, 938 runs total).** Top group: PRIMARY recipes (Flash-RAER baseline
> + CBR variants). Middle group: ABLATIONS isolating each Flash-RAER
> ingredient + mask criterion + CBR weight form. Bottom group:
> FALSIFIED — pre-registered on-policy alternatives (stochastic q_φ,
> REINFORCE) and full multi-head matching, none of which beat the
> deterministic top-K mask in 5-seed paired-t. Two transferable
> methodological findings emerge: (a) on-policy mechanisms designed for
> autoregressive distillation do not transfer to single-step graph fraud
> detection, (b) 4 of 5 ingredients in the v3.3 Flash-RAER design are
> empirically vacuous when ablated individually.

### Figure 6 (`c3_cbr/fig6_c3_mechanism`) — C3 anti-overcorrection mechanism

**Suggested caption**:

> **C3 — CBR operates as anti-overcorrection regularization (K2 K-cell
> mechanism analysis).** **(a)** Differential waste-shrinkage: across all
> 8 cells, the CBR-trained student reduces "wasted budget" (student
> intervention on nodes where the teacher barely intervenes, dark red)
> by 10-35%, while reducing "useful budget" (intervention on
> high-sensitivity nodes, dark grey) only 0-14%. The mechanism is
> *shrinkage on low-sensitivity nodes*, not the originally-hypothesized
> *reallocation* to high-sensitivity nodes — empirically observed via
> mechanism analysis after the K1 benchmark and disclosed openly.
> **(b)** Mechanism prerequisite: CBR engages only when the teacher
> exhibits differential sensitivity. Amazon-GCN's teacher has median
> sensitivity 0.15 (high-sensitivity fraction = 0%) → CBR has no budget
> to redistribute, explaining the K1 null at p = 0.115 for that cell;
> Amazon-SAGE's teacher is uniformly saturated → CBR shrinks uniformly
> with no differential effect (K1 p = 0.45).

## How to regenerate

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/build_paper_figures.py
```

Reads from `artifacts/tables/` and `artifacts/results/`, writes to
`paper_figures/`. Idempotent. Runtime ~10 s on a laptop. All paths inside
the script are project-relative; no global state.
