\section*{Key Counterexample}

For the AUPRC proposition, consider one positive and one negative node. The
teacher scores the positive at $0.5001$ and the negative at $0.5000$, giving
perfect ranking and AUPRC $1$. A student that scores the positive at $0.4999$
and the negative at $0.5000$ is arbitrarily close in score space but reverses
the ranking and can have AUPRC $0.5$. Thus bounded logits, bounded residuals,
and mask cardinality do not imply an AUPRC capture lower bound.

\section*{Before / After Logic}

\textbf{\textcolor{critical}{Before.}} OPD-Flash is claimed as the first OPD
framework for graph anomaly detection, with a REINFORCE-style gradient identity
and an AUPRC capture proposition.

\textbf{\textcolor{safe}{After required revision.}} Either implement a real
student-induced policy objective, or rename the contribution to a
student-adaptive masked multi-head distillation/compression method. The AUPRC
claim should be empirical, or a conditional ranking-stability lemma with an
explicit teacher margin assumption.

\section*{Recommended Safe C3 Claim}

We introduce Flash-RAER, a contract-preserving lightweight distillation
procedure for RAER/LREE teachers. Flash-RAER trains a 4--5K parameter student
adapter with entropy/disagreement-selected training nodes and auxiliary
matching of teacher final logits, relation contributions, gates, and
prototype-state projections, while preserving inference-time base-freeze,
score-blind evidence inputs, train-only prototype construction, and bounded
residual intervention.

\section*{Acceptance Gate}

The acceptance gate fails: open fatal and critical issues remain, and a concrete
counterexample invalidates the stated AUPRC capture proposition. No target
design files were edited by this audit.
